## Project Notes

The capture-plan hook processes LLM responses in three places:
1. Injected SDK responses (for testing)
2. After successful research 
3. Fallback enrichment when research fails

All three needed the markdown wrapper stripping applied. The stripMarkdownWrapper function uses simple regex patterns that are anchored and safe from ReDoS attacks. The patterns handle optional language specifiers (like ```markdown or ```typescript) and optional newlines.
